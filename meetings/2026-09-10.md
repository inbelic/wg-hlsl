## Meeting Notes

### This Week in HLSL

This week had 29 PRs merged in HLSL.

Work on atomics and textures continued in HLSL. We saw various cleanups and bug
fixes in both the DirectX and SPIR-V backends, and the offload test suite had
the usual test enablement churn.

There were 27 new PRs opened, and there are 58 PRs currently ongoing.

For a full list of merged PRs, see the appendix.

### Requests for Review

No requests for review this week.
